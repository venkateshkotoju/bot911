## ⚠️ LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

Before going live, ensure compliance:

1. **FTC Disclosure** — Add affiliate disclosure to site footer and product pages
   - Required text: *"This site contains affiliate links. We may earn a commission when you purchase through our links at no extra cost to you."*
   
2. **Amazon Associates** — Must display: *"[Site Name] is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program..."*

3. **Privacy Policy** — Must mention use of affiliate tracking cookies

4. **Terms of Service** — Each affiliate program has specific rules about:
   - Not bidding on brand keywords in paid ads
   - Not using coupon/cashback sites
   - Proper disclosure requirements
