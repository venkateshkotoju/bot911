## Email Design Guidelines

### Visual Elements:
- Use emoji strategically (1-2 per subject line)
- Include product images when relevant
- Maintain consistent ModBot 911 branding
- Mobile-responsive design
- Clear call-to-action buttons

### Tone & Voice:
- Enthusiastic but professional
- Technical when needed, accessible always
- Community-focused
- Results-oriented
- Trustworthy and transparent

### Best Practices:
- Personalize with user's name and car model
- Include social proof and testimonials
- Segment based on user behavior and interests
- A/B test subject lines and CTAs
- Track engagement and optimize accordingly
